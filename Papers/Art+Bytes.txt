

        Art and Bytes
         Sleepy 2026

To me, there has always been a close
correlation between well written software
and fine art.

On one end you have artist, who live on 
turning mistakes into a masterpiece. Art 
is not one dimentional, it is up to the 
person experiencing it to understand the 
differnt blending techniques and visual 
styles that at one point in time were 
thought to be "Different", so they were 
pushed aside for years.

Take Da Vinci for instance... He lived a 
highly unconventional lifestyle and died
with 0 fame. It was not until over 200 
years after he died, that his lifes work
the Mona Lisa was put into a kings chambers.
Actually, it was the "chambers" aka the 
bathroom. 

You have to think... The renaissance was 
full of art that was "fine lined" and normally
followed the same principles. 

Then you have Code, which is built more similar
to fine art than you may think.

Art is layers on layers to get the right look. 
Well Code is even more layers to get the right 
look. 

When writing tight loops or pretty much anything
it is good to have a general plan of what your 
going to even do, but typically the power comes
in freestyling.

When freestyling code and just writing as you go,
you may tend to suprise yourself. Sometimes our 
logical brain ignores intuition in seek of other 
answers. Intuition and what your brain "thinks" 
will work might lead you to some shocking things.

You might start writing code, fall asleep, wake up
and see wtf was I doing? And realize you created 
something your awake brain has to put together.

Thats where I got the name Sleepy. Because some of
the best work comes from your soul when your body 
feels to tired to carry on.

Dont be afraid to do something different...

-Sleepy
